Observation skill

How to read this. Every observation is stored with the price at that moment, and after 30, 120 and 300 seconds the move that actually followed is appended. Hit rate is how often the market went the way the observation pointed. Lower bound is the cautious version of that figure — it drops on small samples, so three hits out of three will not outrank six hundred out of a thousand. OOS is the hit rate on data not used to set the weight. If it disagrees with the column beside it, the signal is fitting noise rather than an edge, so that feature's weight is cut. Weights move gradually (35% of the way to target), so one strange day cannot flip the bot.
