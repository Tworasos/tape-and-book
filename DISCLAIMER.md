# Disclaimer

**This software is for research and education. It is not investment advice.**

- The bundled bot trades **paper money only**. It cannot place a real order.
- Nothing here has been shown to be profitable. The repository includes a
  measured result where the baseline rule **loses money before costs** — that
  is kept deliberately, because a tool that only reports success is useless.
- Leveraged trading can lose more than the amount deposited. The demo defaults
  (10x, compounding) are chosen to make mechanics visible, **not** because they
  are prudent.
- Backtests and paper results systematically overstate live performance.
  Fees, slippage, queue position, latency and outages all work against you.
- The authors accept no liability for any loss arising from use of this
  software. If you connect it to real capital, that decision and its
  consequences are entirely yours.
